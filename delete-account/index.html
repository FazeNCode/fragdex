Delete your account

FragDex by WrenchsUp Inc.

In the app

  1. Open FragDex and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email fragdex@wrenchsup.com from the email address on your FragDex account with the subject "Delete my FragDex account". We'll confirm the request and delete your account and its data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address and sign-in details
  • Your collector profile, handle, avatar and showcase photos
  • Your collection, wishlist, ratings, reviews and posts
  • Your XP, level, coins, quests, achievements, streaks and step totals
  • Your follows, friends and any finds you shared on the map

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: copies in routine backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Anonymous scan records: label text and matches that no longer identify you may be kept to improve bottle recognition.
  • Purchase records: Apple, Google and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, keep their own transaction records as the law requires.

Delete some data, keep your account

You can remove showcase photos, delete your reviews and posts, and turn off Share finds on the map (which deletes everything you shared) at any time in the app. To ask us to delete anything else, email fragdex@wrenchsup.com.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel FragDex Pro. Cancel it first: on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, on Android in the Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
