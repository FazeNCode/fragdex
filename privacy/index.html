FRAGDEX

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

FragDex is a fragrance collecting app for iPhone and Android operated by WrenchsUp Inc., 1249 Christie Cir, Milton, Ontario L9T 6V4, Canada ("we", "us"). This policy explains what FragDex collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account information. Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google and choose to share it. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we receive only Apple's private relay address.

Profile and collection. Your collector handle, avatar and cosmetics, the fragrances you add, own, wear or wishlist, your XP, level, coins, quests, achievements and streaks.

Showcase photos. If you add a photo from your library to your collector showcase, we store that photo. It is shown only to collectors who can see your profile, and you can replace or remove it at any time.

Social activity. The collectors you follow and who follow you, friends and friend requests, likes, and the reviews, ratings and posts you write. Your handle, avatar, leaderboard standing, reviews and posts are visible to other collectors, and so is a PRO badge if you subscribe to FragDex Pro. Your collection, stats and achievements are visible to others only if your profile is public or they are your friend.

Scans. When you scan a bottle, the photo is processed on your device and stays on your device. To identify the bottle, we receive the text read from its label and a numeric "feature print" computed from the photo; the photo itself is never uploaded. On Android, the photo also stays on your device and nothing is computed from it; you confirm the fragrance by searching the catalogue. We keep a record of each scan (the text, the candidates offered and the fragrance you chose) to improve recognition, and a confirmed match's feature print helps recognise that bottle for other collectors. None of this shows your face or surroundings, and none of it is shown to other users.

Location, only if you turn on "Share finds on the map". When sharing is on, we store the approximate area (a grid cell about 1 km wide) and date of a fragrance you scan. Other collectors see it on the Find map with your handle, with the area rounded to about 50 km; your friends see the 1 km area. While your profile is private, only your friends see it. Your precise location is used on your device to draw your own map and is never sent to us. Turning sharing off deletes everything you have shared.

Steps. With your permission, FragDex reads your step count from your iPhone's motion sensor, or on Android from your phone's step sensor while FragDex is open (the "Physical activity" permission), and sends daily step totals to us to award XP.

Purchases. If you subscribe to FragDex Pro, Apple processes the payment on iPhone and Google Play processes it on Android. We receive the purchase status and expiry date from Apple or Google through our subscription provider, RevenueCat. We never receive your card details.

Bottle details and lists (FragDex Pro). If you add details for a bottle you own (size, price paid, purchase date, batch code, notes) or make lists of fragrances, we store them so they sync across your devices. They are private: no other collector can see them.

Weather, only if location is already on. For “What to wear today”, if you have already allowed location for the map, FragDex sends a rough position (rounded to about 11 km) to Open-Meteo, a free weather service, to get today's temperature and rain. It never asks for location just for this, and the position is not sent to us or stored.

Reports and blocks. If you report or block another collector or a post, we store the report or block.

How we use it

To run FragDex: to sign you in, save and sync your collection, identify bottles you scan, show leaderboards, friends and reviews, apply purchases, prevent cheating and abuse, and review reports. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run FragDex on our behalf, under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and hosting.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose them, and App Store and Google Play payments.
  • Open-Meteo: today's weather, from a rough position, only if location is on (see above).
  • Mapbox: map display. Mapbox receives technical information such as your IP address and map requests.
  • Amazon Web Services: fragrance images.

Other collectors see only what is described above as visible to them. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account, which permanently deletes your account and the data linked to it from our live systems immediately. Any copies in routine backups are overwritten within 30 days. Anonymous scan records that no longer identify you may be kept to improve recognition. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID subscription settings. On Android, cancel a Google Play subscription in the Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. If you can no longer open the app, email fragdex@wrenchsup.com from your account's email address and we will delete your account and its data for you.

Children

FragDex is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to how it is used. Email fragdex@wrenchsup.com to make a request, and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the app.
